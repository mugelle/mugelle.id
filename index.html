Molded by Hand, Inspired by Nature

MUGELLE grew out of a small kitchen table dream and a deep love for intentional living. Every single mug is thrown, trimmed, shaped, and glazed individually in our boutique studio.

We intentionally celebrate the gentle variance in our shapes—the slight organic wobble of a rim, the unique path of a glaze drip. Inspired by English cottage gardens and slow, sunlit mornings, these pieces are made to be held warmly in both hands.
